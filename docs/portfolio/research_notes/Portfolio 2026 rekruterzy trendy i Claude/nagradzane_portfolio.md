# Award-winning and top-featured personal portfolio sites, late 2025 to October 2026 (research notes for Kamil Kochanowski's new portfolio)

Research date: 2026-10-29. Scope: art directors, graphic designers, motion designers and small studios first, developers second; Central European authors included where found. Sites already in `/home/user/figma/docs/portfolio/LINKI.md` are not re-listed except where they have a new 2026 award or version (marked "already in LINKI").

Screenshots (40 JPG, q75; desktop 1440x900, mobile 390x844 @2x) are in `/home/user/figma/docs/portfolio/research_notes/Portfolio 2026 rekruterzy trendy i Claude/screens/nagrody/`. Naming: `<site>_d.jpg` desktop first screen, `<site>_m.jpg` mobile, `<site>_i-<state>.jpg` interaction state. All file names are repeated next to each URL in Question 2.

Method note (applies to every "measured" statement below): pages were loaded in headless Chromium (Playwright, software WebGL, outbound proxy) on 2026-10-29. "First-load transfer" = sum of response sizes seen in the first ~15-40 s; video is streamed so media totals are partial; time metrics are inflated by software rendering and are therefore not quoted except where stated. Stack = DOM signatures, response headers and library signatures found in downloaded scripts; minified or renamed libraries can be missed. Award data comes from the award sites themselves (CSS Design Awards, CSS Winner and FWA were readable; Awwwards was not reachable and is cited through search-engine result snippets).

## Question 1. Which personal or portfolio sites won or were nominated between late 2025 and October 2026 at the listed award programmes, and which are featured on curated galleries?

### Takeaway
CSS Design Awards (CSSDA) is the only major programme I could read in full: 432 Websites of the Day (WOTD) between 2025-08-22 and 2026-10-28, with the top personal-site results being Bruno Simon (Best Portfolio Site of 2025), Merouane Bali (Best Innovation Site 2025), Long Hoang (8.96, the highest-scoring personal WOTD in the window) and, for art directors, Artemii Lebedev (CSSDA Solo of the Year 2025) and ET Studio (Studio of the Year 2025). Awwwards (via search snippets) rewards the same circle (NOTHIN', Lama Lama, Pacôme Pertant, Léo Parpeix, Jesper Landberg) and the FWA is closing its current site in the first week of November 2026.

### Inferences
- The programmes that matter for a hand-coded personal site (CSSDA, CSS Winner, Awwwards) increasingly reward both studios and solo "creative developer/designer" sites; the CSSDA DOTY results (a solo art director and an independent studio as winners) show that a restrained AD site can win at the top level.
- A Polish presence in these lists exists but is mostly studios (Moyra, RabenRifaie, Riotters, Widelab); I found no Polish solo art-director portfolio among 2025-26 WOTDs, so a Polish AD portfolio would be rare in the lists.
- The current FWA site closing (a new FWA site takes over) and Godly's rename to Recent mean the "gallery landscape" is shifting; submission strategy should lean on CSSDA, CSS Winner and Awwwards, plus Recent and Minimal Gallery for curated visibility.

## Question 3. Patterns: what is new compared with 2024-2025, what juries reward, what is overused, and which approaches suit a print, packaging and motion art director who uses AI in production

### Takeaway
Award-level portfolios in 2026 split into two lanes: high-concept 3D/game worlds that collect the innovation scores (Long Hoang 8.96, Bruno Simon) and restrained, fast, typographic art-director or studio sites that win the solo and studio titles (Artemii Lebedev, ET Studio). What is new since 2024-25 is the visible AI assistant, print and physical-object metaphors, status/HUD strips and two-speed toggles; what is overused is the preloader or enter gate, the sound gate, default type choices and OS/terminal metaphors.

### Cited Findings

#### What juries reward (outcome evidence; no jury comments were accessible)
- Awwwards weights Design 40%, Usability 30%, Creativity 20%, Content 10% and needs at least 18 jurors with outliers dropped; CSSDA scores UI, UX and Innovation and treats the 8.0 WOTD threshold as flexible (19% of 2025-26 WOTDs are below 8.0) — [Hon Tran, jury member](https://www.hontran.dev/blog/awwwards-judging-criteria), [CSSDA FAQ](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/long-hoang/50243/), [CSSDA WOTD list](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/wotd-award-winners)
- CSSDA's 2025 year-end picks reward both lanes: Best Portfolio Site Bruno's Portfolio (a 3D driving game, per the earlier LINKI/RESEARCH notes) and Best Innovation Site Merouane Bali (a WebGL site that opens with an "Enter" gate), but Best UI Site went to a typeface site (Exat Typeface), Solo of the Year to Artemii Lebedev (Webflow, video plus index list, 0.89 MB first load) and Studio of the Year to ET Studio (serif wordmark, Nuxt) — [CSSDA WOTY 2025](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/blog/2025-website-of-the-year-winners/430/), [CSSDA DOTY 2025](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/blog/2025-doty-winners-are-here/429/), [merodev.net](https://merodev.net/), [artemiilebedev.com](https://artemiilebedev.com/)
- WebGL is not fading with the juries: among CSSDA WOTD winners (tags chosen by submitters) the WebGL tag appeared on 30% of winners in Q3 2025 (n=40), 25% in Q4 2025 (n=91), 39% in Q1 2026 (n=90), 37% in Q2 2026 (n=91), 50% in Q3 2026 (n=92) and 71% in October 2026 (n=28); the "animated" tag stays at 67-79%; "typographic" falls from 30% (Q3 2025) to 18% (Q1-Q3 2026); the median jury score rises from 7.98 to about 8.2 — my computation from the CSSDA WOTD pages (432 winners, tags read from 430 detail pages) — [CSSDA WOTD list](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/wotd-award-winners)
- Personal portfolios are a minority of WOTDs: 31 of 432 titles contain "Portfolio" or "Folio" (7%), 40 descriptions mention "portfolio" (9%); agencies are 83 (19%) — my count from the same pages — [CSSDA WOTD list](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/wotd-award-winners)
- Webflow's 2026 awards describe "Creative Excellence" as work with "bold visual design, inventive interactions, distinctive art direction" and "the highest level of execution across typography, motion, layout, and brand expression", and add an "AI Achievement of the Year" — [Webflow Awards](https://webflow.com/webflowconf/awards)
- Awwwards' 2025 annual awards went to a celebrity/brand site (Lando Norris) for Site of the Year and to a developer-led interactive site (Messenger) for Developer Site of the Year; the Independent of the Year is a body of work (Louis Paquet) — [Awwwards winners (via search)](https://www.awwwards.com/annual-awards/winners)

### Inferences
Approaches that fit a print, packaging and motion art director who uses AI in production (my recommendations, derived from the evidence above):
1. **Translate prepress into the interface**: an "X-ray / preflight" toggle on each case study that overlays dieline, bleed and safe area, spot-colour names and CMYK values on the packaging render, modelled on metic.'s X-ray spec overlay; an index styled as a press sheet or job file list (Carter's print-shop idea, Elsa's numbered file index with names like the studio's ZAD_NN folders).
2. **Two speeds with a permanent switch**: a fast "list" mode (index of campaigns with client, role, year, as at Lebedev, Carter, Elsa) and an optional "explore" mode (K95-style ring/spiral of key visuals, or a light 3D pack shot), with the switch always visible (Pacôme, Daiki).
3. **A labelled AI layer**: an "ask the portfolio" assistant grounded only in your own case studies with an explicit disclaimer as at NOTHIN', plus per-project "what the AI did / what I decided" notes; the human-craft statement of Long Hoang shows visitors now look for that distinction.
4. **Performance as a visible quality**: budget under 1 MB for the first screen (14 award-listed sites already do it with Astro, Next or Vite); use Astro islands only for the one interactive piece; consider a small performance/status strip in the style of Carter Ogunsola's HUD instead of a loader.
5. **Reel-first without 40 videos**: one muted 10-20 s loop with a poster image, the rest as stills or on-demand (Lebedev ships 16 video elements in 0.89 MB; Pitchford's 41 video elements are the counter-example).
6. **Typography**: one grotesk plus a monospace for labels is the dominant 2026 pattern (60% single family, 23% mono); a serif display in italics is common but flagged as fatiguing; keep the existing Bodoni Moda/Schibsted pair only if it is a deliberate brand decision.
7. **Make crawlers and LLMs an audience**: K95's hidden project list under a canvas and NOTHIN' FAQ pages written as search questions are cheap ways to keep a visual site discoverable; Anthracite explicitly positions "sites for Google and AI".
8. **Skip what is saturated**: long preloaders, sound gates, OS/terminal metaphors, local-clock strips used as decoration, and default Inter plus Instrument Serif.
9. **Polish differentiation**: Polish winners are studios (Moyra, RabenRifaie, Riotters, Widelab); a Polish solo art-director portfolio built on print and packaging craft would be unusual in the CSSDA and CSS Winner lists (the exception I know of is bychudy.com, Framer Awards 2025 Best Newcomer, already in LINKI — [Framer Awards](https://www.framer.com/awards/)).

### Gaps
- No jury comments or per-criterion Awwwards scores were accessible, so "what juries reward" is inferred from outcomes (winners, tags, scores), not from stated reasons.
- The 55-site sample is drawn from award and gallery lists, so there is no baseline for non-awarded sites; the preloader, sound and HUD counts are therefore descriptive of award-listed sites only.
- A 2024 baseline for tag shares was not collected (the CSSDA window starts in August 2025); "new compared with 2024-2025" relies on the earlier LINKI/RESEARCH material and on absence in earlier lists, not on a measured 2024 sample.
- No data on the cost, latency or accuracy of the AI assistants found on NOTHIN', L.I.S.A. and Cloudstudio; I only observed the interface (the NOTHIN' panel was opened, no question was submitted, in line with the read-only constraint).
