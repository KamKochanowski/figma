# Recruiters, job ads and portfolios for senior graphic designers, art directors, motion designers and AI creatives in Poland (data as of 29 Oct 2026)

*Researcher notes for the report writer. Scope: Poland (Warsaw first), 2025-2026 sources, plus a short international benchmark. Prepared for Kamil Kochanowski's new portfolio site. Earlier work (RESEARCH_PORTFOLIO.md, LINKI.md) was skimmed and is not repeated: it already covers Louis Moss, Flux Academy, Tommy Geoco, Zoe Yasemin and the award-site analysis. Everything below is new. All downloaded content was treated as untrusted data (parsed, never executed). Percentages are my own tabulation unless a source is named.*

---

## 1. What do current Polish job ads (pracuj.pl, rocketjobs.pl, justjoin.it, NoFluffJobs, agency ads) require for AD / senior graphic / brand / motion / creative-lead / AI-creative roles, and how do they ask for the portfolio? (counts, quotes, pay, work mode)

### Takeaway
In a 29 Oct 2026 snapshot of 75 in-scope ads, the portfolio is the main gate (67% of ads mention it, 81% of art-director/creative-director ads, 12% say outright that no portfolio means no consideration), but it is asked for as a link: showreels appear in only 9% (23% of AI-titled roles), Behance in 4%, case studies in 7%, a test task in 8%. Typical Polish "senior" means 4-5 years and "art director" 3 years, with hybrid work in Warsaw dominant (52% hybrid, 33% office, 15% remote) and salary shown in only 36% of ads.

### Cited Findings

#### Method, coverage and limits
- Snapshot date 28-29 Oct 2026. Ads were pulled in full (requirements, responsibilities, offer, salary, work mode, contract type, language) from: pracuj.pl (headless Chromium reading the page's embedded JSON, 202 ads fetched), rocketjobs.pl and justjoin.it (their public candidate API, listings plus per-offer detail, 160 + 125 ads fetched), NoFluffJobs (public search API). Source pages: [pracuj.pl category "Reklama / Grafika / Kreacja / Fotografia"](https://www.pracuj.pl/praca/reklama%20grafika%20kreacja%20fotografia;cc,5026), [rocketjobs.pl design category](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferty-pracy/wszystkie-lokalizacje/design), [justjoin.it candidate API, category ux](https://justjoin.it/api/candidate-api/offers?categories=ux&sortBy=newest&orderBy=descending), [NoFluffJobs design](https://nofluffjobs.com/pl/design).
- theprotocol.it could not be read: it answers with a Cloudflare "Cierpliwosci... / Just a moment" challenge to both curl and headless Chromium (HTTP 403), so it is excluded ([theprotocol.it](https://theprotocol.it/filtry/ux-ui)). Per the brief I did not keep retrying. Agency career pages (for example vml.com) are also behind Cloudflare, so Polish agency ads were taken from where agencies post them (rocketjobs.pl / pracuj.pl): VML, TBWA\ basket, MSL and Publicis Groupe, Omnicom Content Experiences / Omnicom Production, WPP Media, hoh, H2O Creative, Green Parrot, tribe47, Digital Kingdom, Halfing & Petersen, CANAL+, Tylko, CD PROJEKT RED.
- Sample definition ("core sample", n = 75): every ad I could find that day whose title is a target role, de-duplicated across boards (same company + title counted once). Groups: Art Director / Creative Director / Creative Lead (16), Senior / Lead / Brand graphic and design-lead roles (23), Motion Designer / animator / video-motion (10), AI-titled creative roles (26). By board: rocketjobs.pl 42, pracuj.pl 32, justjoin.it 1. This is a census of title matches, not a random sample, and it is above the 40-60 requested because I kept every in-scope ad. The full list with links is in Appendix A.
- Comparison pools: "broad pool" (n = 250 graphic / DTP / AD / motion / AI-titled ads on pracuj.pl 140 + rocketjobs.pl 110; of which mid/junior graphic + DTP n = 164) and "UX/UI adjacent" (n = 95 UX/UI/product-designer ads across the three boards). Classification and counts were done by regex on the ad text; this can over-count (for example "portfolio" also matches a company's own client portfolio, such as MODIVO's "W naszym portfolio znajdują się rozpoznawalne brandy"), so treat percentages as +/- a few points.
- Salary caveat: rocketjobs.pl/justjoin.it convert foreign-currency and hourly offers to PLN per month. Two core ads were originally in USD ([Apex Fractional](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/apex-fractional-llc-ai-graphic-video-designer-apex-fractional-warszawa-design-motion-design): 48,000-96,000 USD per year, B2B; [RemoDevs](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/remodevs-ai-video-creator-warszawa-media-produkcja): 2,500-3,000 USD per month) and three are hourly ([Seven Bison Creative Director](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/seven-bison-creative-director-gdansk-marketing-content-copywriting): 120-160 PLN/h net, umowa o dzieło; [Seven Bison AI Filmmaker](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/seven-bison-ai-filmmaker-gdansk-media-produkcja): 120-180 PLN/h; [tribe47 Motion Designer / 3D Generalist](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/tribe47-motion-designer-3d-generalist-warszawa-design-motion-design): 80-120 PLN/h B2B). These are excluded from the monthly medians below.

#### How the portfolio is asked for (patterns with Polish/English quotes)
- Mentioned in 50 of 75 core ads (67%); on pracuj.pl, where the ad is split into lists, the portfolio sits in the mandatory requirements list in 16 of 32 core ads (50%), in the nice-to-have list in 2 (6%) and only in the apply/responsibilities text in 8 (25%) (own count, Appendix A pracuj.pl rows). In the broad pool 156 of 250 (62%) mention it; on pracuj.pl 61 of 140 (44%) list it as required.
- Explicit "no portfolio, no consideration" wording is in 9 of 75 core ads (12%), same share (30/250, 12%) in the broad pool: CD PROJEKT RED "Candidates should include a portfolio in their application. Applications without a portfolio will not be considered." ([ad](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/cd-projekt-red-brand-art-director-warszawa-marketing-marketing)); XTB "Applications without portfolio won't be considered." ([ad](https://www.pracuj.pl/praca/senior-graphic-designer-warszawa-prosta-67,oferta,1005158761)); SELLIFY "UWAGA: Portfolio jest obowiązkowe – bez niego nie będziemy w stanie ocenić Twoich umiejętności." ([ad](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/sellify-marketplace-graphic-designer-grafik-warszawa-design-grafika-02793512)); SUPERSONIC Food "Wyślij nam link do portfolio – to warunek konieczny." ([ad](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/supersonic-food-junior-mid-graphic-designer-ai-digital-warszawa-design-grafika)); WPP Media (Associate Creative Director) "w pierwszej kolejności czekamy na Twoje portfolio (wymagane) i CV. Możemy prosić Cię o realizację zadania rekrutacyjnego." ### Inferences
- Polish agencies and brands screen on "portfolio + role clarity + agency or in-house campaign years", not on showreels or Behance: a link-based site with a 3-project "start here" lane, one-line role statements and a CV link matches how 8-12% of ads already spell out the process ("3 najlepsze projekty... swój wkład", "2-3 projekty z opisem roli", "link do portfolio w CV").
- For an AD/senior profile the competitive baseline in Poland is modest on paper (3-5 years, B2-C1 English, Adobe + Figma) but the best-paid ads add proof demands: measurable business impact (Foap), performance testing (Tylko), directing 3D/CGI productions (Tylko), 360 campaigns and shoots (VML). A portfolio that shows these explicitly would meet the upper end of the market.
- Kamil's mix (campaign 360, key visuals, packaging/print, Amazon/e-commerce, Meta Ads, After Effects, Blender, Figma) maps onto the senior/lead graphic ads far better than onto AD-only ads: print/packaging terms appear in 52% and Figma in 61% of those ads, while Blender is requested in only about 3% of all core ads. Packaging and print are an under-served differentiator against the many digital-only candidates.
- Pay signals: B2B pay for AD/senior roles clusters around 10,000-14,000 PLN net (outliers 20,000-35,000 at AI-native or foreign-paid firms), below UX/UI B2B (about 17,000-21,000). The upper band is reached by AI-native, performance or foreign-owned employers; "senior AD + AI specialist" positioning is therefore commercially relevant.
- Because only 36% of ads show salary and only about a third of senior ads state years, the site should let a recruiter judge seniority from content (roles, scope, clients) rather than from a number.

- theprotocol.it and NoFluffJobs mostly carry IT; the former was inaccessible (Cloudflare). No Polish agency career pages were read directly (Cloudflare on vml.com); coverage of agencies is via their postings on rocketjobs.pl and pracuj.pl.
- Single-day snapshot; no trend over time (the earlier-published 2025 ads are mostly expired). Regex-based counts may mis-classify a few lines.
- Salary data are thin (36% disclosure), mix gross UoP with net B2B, and several figures are conversions; Hays, Michael Page and Antal 2026 Polish salary guides could not be retrieved (search found only aggregators).
- LinkedIn and Behance (login/403) were not used; ads posted only there are missing.

## 2. Which AI-related requirements are now common, how are they phrased, and are "AI Art Director / AI Creative" roles emerging in Poland (and what do they pay)?

### Takeaway
AI is now a near-default line in Polish creative ads (79% of the 75 core ads, 67% of the traditional AD/senior/motion ones, 51% of the broad graphic pool), but only AI-titled roles name tools (Runway 42%, Midjourney 35%, ElevenLabs/HeyGen 27%, Kling/Veo/ChatGPT 23%, Higgsfield and Firefly 19%, ComfyUI/Nano Banana/Claude 15%); AD and senior ads phrase it as openness, practical use inside a craft-first workflow, or building AI workflows. No role literally titled "AI Art Director" was open, but 26 AI-titled creative roles were (about 35% of the core sample), paying roughly 6,000-15,000 PLN per month B2B, with 20,000-35,000 PLN or USD-based offers at AI-native firms.

## 3. What do recruiters, art directors and creative directors say in 2025-2026 about portfolio review (time, first look, number of projects, case study vs gallery, showreel length, site vs Behance vs PDF, red flags, judging and disclosing AI-assisted work)?

### Takeaway
Reviewers decide in seconds to a minute, favour a few strong projects with context and the candidate's own role over galleries, and are now split on AI: surveyed hiring managers see AI-generated portfolio work (29% in a 1,500-manager US survey) and worry about authenticity (86%), while portfolio advice and the best Polish ads converge on "disclose briefly, show your decisions". Polish-language, data-backed recruiter statements are scarce; the best Polish evidence is the ads themselves (Section 1).

### Cited Findings
- Time spent: Wieden+Kennedy's director of creative recruiting, Lauren Ranke: "I spend about five to twenty seconds deciding whether to look more closely at a portfolio"; if intrigued, managers may spend "fifteen to twenty minutes" ([Shift Media / Cutaway, 24 Apr 2019, older](https://cutaway.shift.io/how-hiring-managers-at-ad-agencies-evaluate-your-portfolio)). A recruiting firm for creatives cites "a recent study of hiring managers reviewing design applications" with an average decision time (resume + portfolio) of 55 seconds (study not named, article undated, apparently 2025) ([Artisan Talent](https://creative.artisantalent.com/why-your-portfolio-is-ignored)). A product-design hiring manager writes that some sources put recruiter review of applications at about six seconds, and that most portfolios are cut within 30 seconds ([Matej Latin via ADPList newsletter](https://adplist.substack.com/p/only-30-seconds-to-reject-your-portfolio); product/UX focus, date not shown). No Polish measurement found.
- What is looked at first: clarity of what you do and a featured project. Jim Bell (creative director, Forge): "put your best thinking in front of me right when I get to your site"; Team One's director of creative services: "Formatting is as important as content. Hiring managers get frustrated with really complicated portfolios. We just don't have time to figure it out." (2019, same source as above). Artisan Talent (article undated): a landing page that is "just a logo, or worse, a wall of thumbnails" wastes the prime real estate; add "a bold headline, a quick intro about your skills or niche, and a featured project to click into" ([Artisan Talent](https://creative.artisantalent.com/why-your-portfolio-is-ignored)).
- How many projects: "at least three-to-four prominent case studies" from the last three to four years, with links to them in a prominent place on the homepage and everything else in the navigation ([Artisan Talent red flags](https://creative.artisantalent.com/portfolio-red-flags)); "short, scannable case studies for 2-3 projects" covering challenge, role, process, outcome ([Artisan Talent](https://creative.artisantalent.com/why-your-portfolio-is-ignored)). Polish ads: BlockWise asks for the 3 best projects with own contribution; Seven Bison 2-3 projects with role; SuperVista min. 3 video ads; Aterima 5-10 AI works; Nexbe 3-5 reels (Section 1).
- Case study vs gallery: "If your portfolio is just screenshots with no context, it's getting skipped" and reviewers want challenge, role, process and what changed ([Artisan Talent](https://creative.artisantalent.com/why-your-portfolio-is-ignored)); "I see a lot of portfolios that have one piece out of a [whole] campaign" and everyone wants to see how you work with others to reach the outcome; a case study page should have brief, team members, responsibilities and outcome ([Artisan Talent red flags](https://creative.artisantalent.com/portfolio-red-flags)). ADPList/Latin: a case study is "a movie trailer", aim for a 10-minute read, text-to-visual ratio about 40/60, use STAR, write "I" not "we", give descriptive titles, avoid pixelated images and spelling errors ([ADPList](https://adplist.substack.com/p/only-30-seconds-to-reject-your-portfolio)).
- Attribution is a hiring criterion at the top of the market: Pentagram's London brief for a midweight designer (Oct 2026): "We'd like a mix of commercial brand work, conceptual work, and things you made for yourself. For every project, tell us plainly what you did and what other people did. We would rather see honest attribution on a rough project than a polished one where we can't understand your involvement." plus a 150-word question "Show us one project where the system did something you hadn't fully planned for" ([Pentagram posting PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Sd5yBWonxS3XmxXg9QMWtR2FxYyfVKBb/view); listed on [pentagram.com/careers](https://www.pentagram.com/careers)).
- Showreel length: sources I could open do not give a 2025-2026 recruiter survey on reel length. Search snippets (not opened, treat as unverified): a Fueler article dated Oct 2026 argues motion reels should be under 60 seconds ([Fueler](https://fueler.io/blog/motion-designer-portfolio-showreel-under-60-seconds)); Creative Bloq quoted The Mill's Claire Anderson advising roughly 1.5-2 minutes (older) ([Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/audiovisual/perfect-showreel-top-tips-9134570)); in 2019 Forge's creative director said a reel that takes "a minute or two to build" loses him ([Shift Media](https://cutaway.shift.io/how-hiring-managers-at-ad-agencies-evaluate-your-portfolio)). Contrarian ad evidence: Viktor.com "We will ask for links, dates and project files rather than a reel, because a reel cannot tell us what you personally cut. Your reel is six beautiful pieces from four years." ([ad](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/viktor-com-senior-video-editor-performance-creative--warszawa-design-motion-design)).
- Personal site vs Behance vs PDF: the only quantified source is a Hover survey of 121 people involved in hiring (published by Format; undated, respondents mostly Hover domain owners, so skewed to websites): 86% agree they will visit a portfolio when given the option and 71% agree portfolio quality influences hiring; 38% agreed a portfolio site is important, 31% neutral, 28% disagreed ([Format / Hover](https://www.format.com/magazine/how-to-create-portfolio-hired-design-jobs)). Polish ads accept any link (Green Parrot: LinkedIn, online card, portfolio or Behance; Commerce-ui: "any relevant links or portfolio"; Nexbe: links not large attachments; SELLIFY: link to a portfolio page or to files); PDFs are rarely mentioned (Section 1). Pentagram asks for "Portfolio and CV" by email or a Google form; Buck: "send an email with a link to your work" ([Buck careers](https://buck.co/careers)).
- Red flags named by recruiters: old or irrelevant work (older than 3-4 years), single pieces without campaign context, disorganised layout ("Readers will be leaving after two clicks!"), no contact information, a non-responsive site, load time beyond one second, and a COVID-era "most recent project" ([Artisan Talent](https://creative.artisantalent.com/portfolio-red-flags), [Artisan Talent](https://creative.artisantalent.com/why-your-portfolio-is-ignored)); "a lot of hiring teams are reviewing on their phones, between meetings, or on the train home"; generic or template-driven portfolios (search snippet; source not opened). From the ADPList piece: pixelated or tiny images, grammar mistakes, project/company name as case-study title, overuse of "we", cookie-cutter structure.
- Recency and freshness: Artisan "refresh your portfolio every quarter" ([Artisan Talent](https://creative.artisantalent.com/why-your-portfolio-is-ignored)); a Polish agency employer asks for work that shows scope of responsibility, not only concept (Agencja hoh "Masz portfolio pokazujące realne projekty i Twój zakres odpowiedzialności" ([ad](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/agencja-hoh-art-director-warszawa-design-grafika)).
- How AI-assisted work is judged: a Resume Genius survey of 1,500 US hiring managers and hiring-team members (2026 report; general hiring, not creative-only): 80% have encountered AI-assisted applications (only 19% have not), 58% have received AI-generated resumes or cover letters, 29% "have received AI-generated portfolio projects or creative work", 28% caught people using AI to cheat on online skills assessments, 86% agree AI "will create new challenges in assessing whether a candidate's resume, portfolio, interview responses... accurately reflect their abilities" and 82% are concerned about candidates using AI. The report's "35% always disclose" figure concerns employers disclosing their own AI use in hiring, not candidates ([Resume Genius](https://resumegenius.com/blog/job-hunting/ai-impact-on-hiring-2026)). The report authors conclude that employers appear "more concerned with maintaining confidence that a candidate's qualifications, work samples, and interview performance genuinely represent their knowledge" than with AI use itself (same source).
- Should candidates disclose AI use? Portfolio-platform advice (vendor, 3 Oct 2026): disclose when AI played a meaningful role and someone could reasonably wonder how the work was made; "One clear sentence can often provide enough context"; answer where AI helped, what you did, who made the final decisions; place it inside the relevant case study, not only on About; example: "AI was used to explore early visual directions. I selected the final concept, rebuilt the composition, refined the visual system, and prepared the final assets." ([Fueler](https://fueler.io/blog/how-to-show-ai-assisted-work-in-your-portfolio-honestly); Fueler sells portfolio software, so treat as opinion). Polish hiring practice matches this: Apex "one line each on what the tool did and what you did"; BlockWise "Chcemy zobaczyć Twoje decyzje, nie tylko wynik generatora"; Seven Bison "Liczy się przede wszystkim jakość i realizm efektów, niezależnie od tego, jakim narzędziem zostały wygenerowane" (the tool does not matter, the result does) (Section 1/2). Aquent (search snippet only, page not retrievable) states that creatives who leave recent AI-assisted work off their portfolio risk being overlooked ([Aquent](https://aquent.com/en-gb/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/6999)).
- Professional sentiment: Creative Boom's June 2026 survey (882 creatives, UK/US weighted, 43% with more than a decade of experience): 86% use AI tools, only 10% rate AI's overall effect on the industry as positive ([Creative Boom](https://creativeboom.com/news/the-state-of-the-creative-industry-2026-what-our-survey-tells-us-about-money-burnout-and-ai)).
- The question of whether the portfolio itself survives AI is being researched: creative director Tim Brunelle launched a "2026 Creative Director Survey" (20 Jul 2026; closed 14 Aug) as a follow-up to a 2010 survey in which 66% crowned the ability to curate the most important skill; results had not been published in what I could find ([Tim Brunelle, Substack](https://timbrunelle.substack.com/p/2026-creative-director-survey)).
- Polish-language guidance is generic and older: aplikuj.pl says a portfolio can be a website, PDF or multimedia presentation and should show process and the final effect ([aplikuj.pl](https://www.aplikuj.pl/cv/portfolio-wazny-dodatek-do-cv/), undated); a Polish Sii blog guide for UX/UI portfolios warns that old designs look dated (search snippet only, [Sii](https://sii.pl/blog/en/?p=24935)). A Muzli UX portfolio guide dated March 2026 claims an initial review of 2-3 minutes (search snippet, not opened) ([Muzli](https://muz.li/blog/how-to-build-a-ux-portfolio-that-actually-gets-you-hired-2026/)).
- Already covered in the earlier research file (not repeated): Louis Moss ("How Art Directors ACTUALLY Review Portfolios", 5-8 projects, "kill your favourites"), Flux Academy, Tommy Geoco, Karol Stefański's Polish video "10 koszmarnych błędów w portfolio designera", GraphiqVibe ([Louis Moss video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VrEc7yIucRQ), [Karol Stefański](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-o82iQxAMuY)). This session could not re-open YouTube (HTTP 429 from the server IP), so no new video titles/chapters were extracted.

### Inferences
- The most consistent cross-source rules: (1) reviewers need a one-screen answer to "who is this and what do they do", (2) 3-4 featured case studies with role, team and outcome, (3) recent work (last 3-4 years), (4) no friction (fast, mobile-first, working links, visible contact), (5) honest attribution, (6) AI use disclosed in one precise sentence per project.
- Evidence strength is weak for numbers (5-20 seconds, 55 seconds and 6 seconds come from different populations and eras) and better for qualitative patterns. A "60-second recruiter lane" is a safe design target.
- AI disclosure is both a risk control (29% of US hiring managers have seen AI-generated portfolio work; 86% expect assessment problems) and an asset (Polish AI-titled ads explicitly request AI examples). A per-project "AI role" line fits both.
- Contradictory stance exists at the top of the market (Pentagram Team Lupi does not use AI for concepts; Monks/R/GA are AI-native), so the AI section should state principles (AI for exploration, production speed and tooling; decisions human) that are compatible with both camps.

### Gaps
- No Polish recruiter or creative-director survey, interview or HR-blog piece with concrete numbers (time per portfolio, red flags) was found in 2025-2026; pracuj.pl, rocketjobs.pl and justjoin.it blog searches returned nothing relevant. Hays, Michael Page and Antal Poland guides were not retrievable.
- YouTube returned HTTP 429, so video descriptions and chapters were not read this session; LinkedIn and Medium posts were not accessible by search for creative-director opinions in 2025-2026.
- Several time and reel-length claims come only from search snippets (Format "3 seconds", Fueler "<60 s", Creative Bloq/The Mill, Muzli "2-3 minutes", Aquent); they are flagged and should not be quoted as verified.
- The Tim Brunelle creative-director survey results were not published at the time of search.

## 5. What does all of this imply for the portfolio website (must-have sections, fast lane for recruiters, CV/PDF, contact, PL/EN, proof of results, AI-skills evidence)?

### Takeaway
Design for a 30-60 second recruiter pass first: a visible role line, three featured projects with role and result, a CV link and contact within one scroll, English by default with a Polish version, and AI shown as dated, attributed workflow examples rather than a gimmick; the discovery layer can stay distinctive but must not block this lane.

### Cited Findings
- Reviewers decide in seconds (5-20 s at W+K, about 55 s average for resume + portfolio, about 6 s application screens in another source) and dislike complicated navigation ([Shift Media 2019](https://cutaway.shift.io/how-hiring-managers-at-ad-agencies-evaluate-your-portfolio), [Artisan Talent](https://creative.artisantalent.com/why-your-portfolio-is-ignored), [ADPList](https://adplist.substack.com/p/only-30-seconds-to-reject-your-portfolio)).
- Polish ads ask for a link to the portfolio inside the CV/application, plus 2-3 projects with own role; 12% say no portfolio means no consideration; showreel 9%, Behance 4%, case study 7%, test task 8% (Section 1; [BlockWise](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/blockwise-art-director-warszawa-design-grafika), [Seven Bison](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/seven-bison-creative-director-gdansk-marketing-content-copywriting), [CD PROJEKT RED](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/cd-projekt-red-brand-art-director-warszawa-marketing-marketing)).
- English is mentioned in 94% of AD ads (level stated in 63%, typically B2-C1) while many Polish agencies also write in Polish (Section 1; [Foap](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/foap-creative-director-warszawie-marketing-content-copywriting-4e699888), [Seven Bison](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/seven-bison-creative-director-gdansk-marketing-content-copywriting)).
- Proof-of-results asks: "measurable business impact" (Foap), "creative testing, iterations" (Tylko), "Portfolio... pokazuje... craft wykonania" (Publicis), "realne projekty i Twój zakres odpowiedzialności" (Agencja hoh) ([Tylko](https://www.pracuj.pl/praca/art-director-warszawa-czerska-8-10,oferta,1005170412), [Agencja hoh](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/agencja-hoh-art-director-warszawa-design-grafika)).
- AI evidence asks: "2 to 3 examples where AI did part of the work, one line each on what the tool did and what you did" (Apex), "krótkie pokazanie drogi od materiału wyjściowego do finalnego efektu" (BlockWise), disclosure in one sentence near the project (Fueler) ([Apex](https://rocketjobs.pl/oferta-pracy/apex-fractional-llc-ai-graphic-video-designer-apex-fractional-warszawa-design-motion-design), [Fueler](https://fueler.io/blog/how-to-show-ai-assisted-work-in-your-portfolio-honestly)).
- Red flags that a site can control: slow load (beyond one second), no responsive layout, no visible contact, old work, project-only titles, "we" instead of "I", low-resolution images ([Artisan Talent](https://creative.artisantalent.com/portfolio-red-flags), [ADPList](https://adplist.substack.com/p/only-30-seconds-to-reject-your-portfolio)).
- Top-studio benchmark: honest attribution and breadth of thinking (Pentagram, R/GA), AI as medium or tooling (R/GA, Monks) versus not for concepts (Pentagram) (Section 4).

### Inferences
Recruiter fast lane and structure
1. Above the fold, no loading screen: name, one-line role ("Art Director / senior graphic and motion designer, AI-enabled production, Warsaw"), availability line (UoP / B2B, hybrid Warsaw or remote), and two buttons: "Portfolio (3 projects)" and "CV PDF (PL / EN)", plus email and LinkedIn. This matches ads that want a link in the CV and 2-3 named projects.
2. Featured set of three case studies (the number requested by BlockWise, Seven Bison and implied by Artisan's 3-4), each opening with: one-line outcome, client, year, your role, team, channels, deliverables, then Brief / Idea / Decisions / Result (numbers) / "AI: what the tool did, what I did". Use "I" statements; show 2-3 process frames, not only finals; put recent work (last 3-4 years) first. Keep an archive of 5-8 more projects for depth, not a large gallery.
3. Pick the three to cover the market's modal asks: (a) a 360 campaign with key visual and cross-channel roll-out (CANAL+, VML, Armatura, H2O), (b) a packaging/print/prepress project with production detail (print terms in 52% of senior graphic ads, 27% of all core ads) and an e-commerce/Amazon project with variants and testing (Tylko, Garett, SELLIFY), (c) a motion/3D piece (After Effects 44%, Blender in 3% so it is a differentiator).
4. A skills block tied to evidence (not logos): Adobe CC, After Effects, Blender, Figma, plus print/prepress, with links to the case study that shows each; add English level (C1 if true) because about 63% of AD ads state a level.
5. Short motion reel (30-60 s) and "project files / breakdowns on request" (Viktor.com's stance, Monks' "programmatic creative" ask), but do not make the reel the front door: only 9% of ads request it (23% of AI-titled).
6. CV/PDF: one A4 CV in PL and EN, with the portfolio URL and QR at the top (VML: "CV which includes a link to your portfolio"; Nexbe: "linki zamiast dużych załączników"). Offer an optional lightweight PDF of the three featured projects (under a few MB) for agencies that pitch decks internally (Publicis asks for a shared Art & Copy portfolio if applying as a pair), but treat the website as the primary asset.
7. Contact and logistics: visible within one click on every page; mention UoP/B2B and Warsaw hybrid; consider a one-line statement of availability and notice period. Keep B2B terms flexible given the new PIP scrutiny of sham B2B (an employer-side consideration).
8. PL/EN: English default with a visible PL switch (Polish agencies write in PL, global ones in EN); keep page URLs stable so shared links work; make sure the CV and portfolio language match the target ad.
9. Proof: clients (They: Vaillant, MOYA, MAPEI, MSD) as logos with outcome lines and numbers; add awards/press if any; use the earlier research's numeric proofs (4K film in 7 days, 14 MAPEI films, 11 languages) as one-line results.
10. AI evidence section ("AI in my workflow"): 3 dated examples with before/after, tools named in the ads' vocabulary (Midjourney, Firefly, Runway, Kling, Veo, Higgsfield, ComfyUI, Nano Banana/Gemini, ElevenLabs, ChatGPT/Claude, n8n/Make), time or cost saved, and an explicit "human decisions" line; plus a short principle statement (AI accelerates craft, art direction stays human). This serves the AI-titled ads (portfolio of 3-10 AI works) and the AD ads that treat AI as a plus.
11. Claude-powered features: keep them optional and below the fast lane, grounded only in the case-study content (to avoid hallucination), and use them to demonstrate workflow skill (for example "ask about this project" or a brief-to-concept demo) rather than as navigation. If used, state clearly that it is an AI assistant.
12. Technical hygiene: fast first paint, mobile-first (hiring teams review on phones), working links, no "Not Supported" fallbacks, visible updated date, refresh quarterly.
